What is included

  • Copy an item to another board with its subitems, updates with replies, and files.
  • A preview that shows what would be lost before anything is written.
  • The workflow action Copy item to board, to copy automatically.
  • Copying by asking monday's AI assistant (Sidekick), by board name.
  • Everyone in your account can use it. There is no limit on copies, boards or items.

How billing works

You subscribe and pay inside monday.com, which handles the payment, the invoices, currencies and taxes. We never see your payment details. The price on this page is the price on the app's monday.com listing; if it changes, the listing changes first and the new price applies from your next billing period.

When the 14 days are over and there is no subscription, the app stops copying and says so in its window. Your items and boards are not touched: the app keeps no copy of your data, and the copies it already made are ordinary items on your boards.
